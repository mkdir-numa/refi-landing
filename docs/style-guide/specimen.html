03Colour

The staking site's paper and ink. Our own sun and grid.

Neutrals carry over unchanged, so the two sites read as one family. Sun gold is the shared thread: the pulse colour on both. The rest of the accents are ReFi Hub's own.

Sun, copper and grid, chosen on 22 October 2026. An EV-led set with a volt green was considered and dropped; the Markdown guide records it.

04Spacing and grid

One container. One rhythm.

A 4px base, a short scale, 64 to 120px between sections, and a single 1200px container so the left edge never jumps.

Scale

Section order on the landing page

FooterInk

No two neighbours share a background. An ink band about every third section. Section padding clamp(64px, 9vw, 120px); head to content clamp(32px, 4vw, 56px).

12 columns · 24px gap · 1200px max · gutter clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px)

Common splits: 7/5 for copy beside art or a readout, 4/8 for a lead number beside tiles, 5/7 for a head beside a list. Radii: 18px for cards and images, 10px for small chips and inputs, 28px for the closing card only, and the pill for buttons.

07Artwork and motif

The same print technique, drawn from the grid.

The staking site's stipple is organic: round dots, polyps, drifting plankton. ReFi Hub's is infrastructure: square dots on a grid, straight runs and cable sags, light that travels and fills. Move your pointer over a drawing.

Decision · lead motif

Recommendation: Grid lines as the lead, Daylight sweep as the section background, Sunfield for one or two small spots.

Staking site · reefReFi Hub · energy
DotRoundSquare, aligned to a grid
GeometryCurves, fronds, petalsStraight runs, right angles, rows of cells, cable sags
MovementDrift, sway, breatheTravel and fill: pulses along a line, a sweep, a meter filling
RhythmIrregular, naturalRegular and periodic, like a meter
ColourSea lines, coral and goldGrid-slate dots, sun pulses, copper for faults
MeaningCorals plantedElectricity produced, carried and paid out

AGrid lines · recommended lead

The business model, drawn.

Six asset nodes, solar panels in dots and EV chargers as dot bars that fill like a battery, joined by sagging power lines to one payout node. Pulses travel from each asset to the node, which flashes as each one lands. The two assets that are behind send pulses less often, in copper.

refiSOL band, record section, Hero B
Later
One pulse per real payment

BDaylight sweep

A solar array from above.

A field of square dots, grouped into panels by thin gutters. A soft band of light crosses it every few seconds, and the dots it touches grow and turn gold. ReFi Hub's version of the staking site's plankton.

CSunfield

A halftone sun over panel rows.

A disc of square dots, largest at the centre, with dotted rays that carry a slow wave outwards. Below, rows of panels in dots, with light sliding across them. The most literal and the most immediately solar.

08Motion and accessibility

Calm, and never in the way.

One moving thing per screen. Content is never hidden waiting for an animation. With reduced motion turned on, every drawing shows one still frame and numbers appear at their final value.

09Do and don't

The short version.

Do
  • Set section headings in Inter 400, tracked -.04em
  • Open every section with a numbered mono kicker
  • Make one number the hero of each band, with its source
  • Put units in mono after Geist numbers
  • Keep one container, 1200px, and one card radius, 18px
  • Use one black, #0F0E0C, for bands and the footer
  • Draw the motif in square dots on a grid
  • Name a problem plainly in a calm notice
  • Keep content visible without JavaScript
Don't
  • Set anything in Inter 700, or bold a heading
  • Open six sections the same way
  • Show six numbers at equal weight
  • Invent in-between sizes like 13.5px or 9.5px
  • Let sections change width, or mix radii
  • Mix #000, #151412 and #141313
  • Use round drifting dots (that is the reef)
  • Bring sea or coral in, except the Stake button and the refiSOL band
  • Hide cards until they scroll into view
