#### FLP Series 1 - Pivot Green — Initial Asset Performance Summary

#### Asset Overview

-   **Location:** Singapore (industrial rooftop solar)
-   **Technology:** Solar PV
-   **Offtaker:** **Freight Links**
-   **Contract:** 24-year on-site electricity offtake
-   **Additional Revenue:** Sale of Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)

#### Asset Status

The asset is operational and selling electricity under a long-term contracted arrangement with the site operator. In addition to electricity sales, the project generates incremental revenue through REC monetization.

#### Cumulative Cash Performance (Baseline)

-   **Capital deployed:** USD 59,000
-   **Total cash received to date:** USD 1,985.90
-   **Number of payments received:** 3
-   **Missed payments:** None

Cash receipts reflect early operating-period performance following commissioning.

#### Revenue Context

Revenue is derived from:

-   Contracted on-site electricity sales, and
-   Sale of associated Renewable Energy Certificates.

Month-to-month variation is expected in operating solar assets and reflects normal operating factors rather than changes to contractual terms.

##### Reporting Transition

This post establishes the baseline performance and operating context for the FLP asset.

**Going forward, asset updates will be published monthly and will report period activity alongside cumulative performance.**
