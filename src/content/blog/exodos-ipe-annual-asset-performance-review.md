Exodos IPE demonstrated stable operating performance over the 2025 calendar year, with uninterrupted cashflows and consistent energy delivery. The asset completed its first full year of operation without payment interruptions, providing a clean performance reference for forward reporting.

#### Reporting Notes

All figures reflect cash received. Performance is asset-specific and subject to operating and environmental conditions. Historical payment data is maintained at the asset level and updated continuously.
