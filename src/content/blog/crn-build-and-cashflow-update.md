#### CRN — Build Update and Cashflow Summary

#### Asset Overview

-   **Location:** Brazil
-   **Technology:** Solar PV
-   **Project Phase:** Transitioning from development to operation

#### Asset Status

Construction is complete and the asset is connected to the grid. Final commissioning and administrative close-out are in progress with the regional grid operator (CELESC). Commercial energy generation has not yet commenced.

#### Cumulative Cash Performance (Baseline)

-   **Capital deployed:** USD 100,000
-   **Total cash received to date:** USD 5,644.80
-   **Number of payments received:** 6
-   **Missed payments:** None

All payments to date were received as scheduled during the development phase.

#### Payment Context

Cash receipts to date represent **interim development-phase payments** and were not derived from energy generation. No energy production has been reported during this period.

#### Reporting Transition

This post establishes the baseline performance and current project status for CRN.

**Going forward, asset updates will be published monthly and will report period activity alongside cumulative performance.**

Future reporting is expected to transition from development-phase payments to operating cashflows once commercial generation begins.

#### Reporting Notes

All figures reflect cash received. Performance is asset-specific and subject to operating and commissioning conditions. Historical payment data is maintained at the asset level and updated continuously.
